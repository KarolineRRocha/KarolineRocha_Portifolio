Hello! I'm Karoline

I'm a web developer in training, fueled by curiosity, purpose, and plenty of good cups of coffee. I like to understand what I'm doing, not just make it work — even if that means investigating bugs like they're part of a suspense series.

💻

My Tech Stack

I work with Angular, Laravel, TypeScript, PHP, HTML, Tailwind CSS, Git, and Vite. I write code like I organize ideas: with logic, clarity, and a bit of stubbornness until everything works (including that one bug that "made no sense at all").

🌱

Beyond the Screen

Off the screen, I nurture other passions: cooking like I'm programming recipes, tending my plants and garden like I'm versioning nature, binge-watching series (preferably without streaming bugs), and enjoying simple moments with my husband — my steady partner, even when the Wi-Fi fails. And whenever I can, I recharge my mind with the smell of the sea — my favorite breakpoint between deploy cycles.

🎯

My Values

I value well-thought-out solutions, intuitive interfaces, and code that my "future self" can read without freaking out. For me, developing is about combining knowledge, creativity, and sensitivity into something that truly makes sense — and, ideally, doesn't break after deployment.
